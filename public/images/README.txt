Place your personal photographs in this folder. Use these exact filenames so
the app loads them automatically:

- zai-1.jpg   (Garden Frame I)
- zai-2.jpg   (Garden Frame II)
- zai-3.jpg   (Garden Frame III)
- my-rose.jpg (Chapter 04: A Rose From India)

JPG, PNG, and WebP files work. For a fast-loading site, use compressed images
around 1200 pixels on the longest edge.

The image paths are hardcoded in the app as `/images/<filename>`. Keep the same
filenames or update the matching paths in `src/data/content.ts`.

图片路径已写在网站中（`/images/<文件名>`）。请使用上面的文件名，网站就会自动显示照片。
